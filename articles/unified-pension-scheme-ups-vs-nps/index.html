Unified Pension Scheme (UPS): How It Differs From NPS

Central Government employees covered by the National Pension System (NPS) on 1 April 2025, and eligible recruits joining on or after that date.Checked against official documents on 4 Oct 2026

Short answerThe Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) is not a separate pension system. It is an option within the National Pension System (NPS) that adds an assured-payout framework, subject to notified conditions. It took effect on 1 April 2025. Employees who choose UPS have a one-time, one-way option to switch back to NPS.

What UPS Is

UPS took effect on 1 April 2025 as an option under NPS for eligible Central Government employees. It introduces an assured-payout framework, subject to notified conditions (DFS/PFRDA, F. No. FX-1/3/2024-PR dated 24.01.2025).

So the key difference is that UPS brings an assured-payout framework for employees who choose it. This page does not give the payout formula or the qualifying conditions. Read them in the notified UPS regulations before you decide.

Who Can Choose UPS

UPS is open to existing Central Government employees who were under NPS on 1 April 2025, and to eligible new recruits joining on or after that date. The option and deadlines are as notified (DFS/PFRDA notification dated 24.01.2025).

Switching Back From UPS to NPS

From 25 August 2025, employees who opted for UPS have a one-time, one-way facility to switch back to NPS (DFS OM 1/3/2024-PR dated 25.08.2025).

The switch must generally be made no later than one year before superannuation, or three months before the deemed date of voluntary retirement. Timing and disqualification conditions in the OM apply (DFS OM dated 25.08.2025). Because the facility is one-time and one-way, plan the decision carefully.

Fixed Medical Allowance for UPS Employees

From 13 March 2026, Central Government employees covered by UPS are eligible for Fixed Medical Allowance on the same terms as the earlier DoPPW orders of 6 December 2023 and 7 February 2025. The current rate is ₹1,000 per month, subject to residence and CGHS or OPD-option conditions (DoPPW OM 04/07/2020-P&PW(D)/6455 dated 13.03.2026).

Where to Check

  • The PFRDA Unified Pension Scheme page, for the notification and implementing regulations (PFRDA UPS page).
  • The DFS OM of 25 August 2025 on switching back to NPS (DFS OM dated 25.08.2025).
  • The DoPPW OM of 13 March 2026 on Fixed Medical Allowance (DoPPW OM dated 13.03.2026).
  • Your Drawing and Disbursing Officer for your option status and deadlines.

Related Questions

When did UPS start?

UPS took effect on 1 April 2025 [(DFS/PFRDA notification dated 24.01.2025)](https://www.pfrda.org.in/en/web/pfrda/schemes/national-pension-system/unified-pension-scheme).

Is UPS a replacement for NPS?

No. UPS operates as an option under NPS [(DFS/PFRDA notification dated 24.01.2025)](https://www.pfrda.org.in/en/web/pfrda/schemes/national-pension-system/unified-pension-scheme).

Can I go back to NPS after choosing UPS?

Yes, once. The switch is one-time and one-way, generally no later than one year before superannuation or three months before the deemed date of voluntary retirement [(DFS OM dated 25.08.2025)](https://www.pfrda.org.in/documents/33652/313350/DFS%2BOM%2Bdtd%2B25082025%2B-%2BOne%2Btime%2Bone%2Bway%2Bfrom%2BUPS%2Bto%2BNPS.pdf).

What is the gratuity ceiling for employees under NPS?

From 1 January 2024 the maximum retirement and death gratuity is ₹25 lakh under both the CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021 and the CCS (Payment of Gratuity under NPS) Rules, 2021 [(DoPPW OM 28/03/2024-P&PW(B)/Gratuity/9559 dated 30.05.2024)](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2024-2025/Office_Order.pdf).
