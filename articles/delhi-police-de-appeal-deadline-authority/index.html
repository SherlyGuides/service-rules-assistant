Delhi Police Departmental Enquiry Appeal: Deadline and Authority

Delhi Police officers of subordinate rank (Constable to Inspector) governed by the Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980.Checked against official documents on 4 Oct 2026

Short answerUnder Rule 24(3) of the Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, file your appeal within 30 days of receiving the punishment order, excluding time taken to get record copies. Submit it through your DCP. Orders of a DCP or ACP go to the Additional Commissioner, and the Additional Commissioner's orders go to the Commissioner.

Which Orders You Can Appeal

Appeals lie against orders of dismissal, removal, reduction in rank or pay, forfeiture of service, fine up to one month's pay, withholding of increment and censure (DP (P&A) Rule 23(1), PDF p. 27). There is only one appeal from the original order, and the appellate order is final (DP (P&A) Rule 23(2), PDF p. 28).

You get a free copy of the appealable order (DP (P&A) Rule 23(3), PDF p. 28). You may apply to the disciplinary authority for a copy of the record to prepare your appeal. Preliminary enquiry records are not given unless they also form part of the departmental proceedings (DP (P&A) Rule 23(4), PDF p. 28).

Who Hears Your Appeal

Rule 23(6) sets the appellate authority according to who passed the original punishment order (DP (P&A) Rule 23(6), PDF p. 28):

Original order passed by Appellate authority
DCP, Addl. DCP, Principal of a Police Training School or College, ACP, or an officer of equivalent rank Additional Commissioner of Police
Additional Commissioner of Police Commissioner of Police
Commissioner of Police Administrator, Delhi

The 30-Day Deadline and How to File

Send your appeal through the DCP of the district or unit where you serve or served (DP (P&A) Rule 24(1), PDF p. 28). It must set out your grounds and enclose a copy of the disciplinary authority's order (DP (P&A) Rule 24(2), PDF p. 28).

An appeal not filed within 30 days of receiving the original order, excluding the time taken to obtain the record copy, is barred by limitation. The appellate authority may accept a late appeal if the delay was due to circumstances beyond your control. If you are believed to be evading receipt, the one month runs from the date the order was sent by registered post AD (DP (P&A) Rule 24(3), PDF p. 28).

In one case the appellate authority dismissed an appeal filed about 11 months after the 30-day limit (Ram Sahai Meena v. Commissioner of Police, Delhi HC, 18.03.2010, PDF p. 3). The Delhi High Court declined to interfere, holding that a bare claim of family problems was not sufficient reason to condone the delay (Ram Sahai Meena, PDF p. 4).

What the Appellate Authority Can Do, and Revision

The appellate authority may confirm the order, accept the appeal and set aside the punishment, reduce it, remit the case for further inquiry, or pass other orders (DP (P&A) Rule 25(1), PDF p. 28) (DP (P&A) Rule 25(1), PDF p. 29). It may enhance the punishment only after a fresh show cause notice and a reasonable opportunity, including a personal hearing if asked for (DP (P&A) Rule 25(1)(d), PDF p. 29). Every appellate order must give reasons, and you get a free copy (DP (P&A) Rule 25(2), PDF p. 29).

If your appeal is rejected, you cannot file a second appeal. You may file a revision within a month of receiving the appellate order, to the authority above the appellate authority, on grounds of material irregularity or illegality (DP (P&A) Rule 25-A, PDF p. 29).

Where to Check

  • Rules 23 to 25-A of the Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, in the India Code booklet (DP (P&A) Rules 23-25, PDF p. 27).
  • The date stamp or receipt on your copy of the punishment order, because the 30 days run from receipt.
  • Civilian staff in Delhi Police are governed by the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965 or their parent department's rules, not these rules (DP (P&A) Rule 3(ii), PDF p. 17).

Related Questions

What is the time limit for a Delhi Police departmental appeal?

30 days from receipt of the original order, excluding time taken to obtain the record copy [(DP (P&A) Rule 24(3), PDF p. 28)](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/2188ca35-06ec-46d7-84ef-210bfa017c71/content#page=28).

Can a late appeal be accepted?

Yes, if the appellate authority finds the delay occurred due to circumstances beyond your control [(DP (P&A) Rule 24(3), PDF p. 28)](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/2188ca35-06ec-46d7-84ef-210bfa017c71/content#page=28). A vague reason may not be enough [(Ram Sahai Meena, Delhi HC, 18.03.2010, PDF p. 4)](https://indian-high-court-judgments.s3.amazonaws.com/data/pdf/year=2010/court=7_26/bench=dhcdb/DLHC011644082009_1_2010-03-18.pdf#page=4).

Is there a second appeal?

No. There is only one appeal [(DP (P&A) Rule 23(2), PDF p. 28)](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/2188ca35-06ec-46d7-84ef-210bfa017c71/content#page=28), but a revision can be filed within a month of the appellate order [(DP (P&A) Rule 25-A, PDF p. 29)](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/2188ca35-06ec-46d7-84ef-210bfa017c71/content#page=29).

Can I appeal against censure?

Yes. Censure is one of the orders against which an appeal lies [(DP (P&A) Rule 23(1), PDF p. 27)](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/2188ca35-06ec-46d7-84ef-210bfa017c71/content#page=27).
