KVS Vacation 2026-27 and Earned Leave for Duty in Vacation

Vacation staff of Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (teachers, librarians, laboratory and Group D staff)Checked against official documents on 4 Oct 2026

Short answerFor 2026-27, most summer-station Kendriya Vidyalayas have a 40 or 49 day summer vacation, a 10 day autumn break from 15 to 24 October 2026, and a winter break. If you are called for duty in a vacation or break for 10 days or more, KVS credits earned leave of 2/5 of those days, within 30 days a year.

Who Gets Vacation in KVS

  • All categories of teachers, librarians, and laboratory assistants, attendants and Group D staff are vacation staff. The Principal and office staff are non-vacation staff. The Vice-Principal post is treated as both (KVS Education Code, Article 66, p. 43).
  • Vacation staff not called for duty during the long (summer or winter) vacation get vacation pay only if they actually worked for at least five months in the preceding academic year (KVS Education Code, Article 67, p. 43).
  • Contractual and part-time teachers are not entitled to vacation pay (KVS Education Code, Article 67(2), p. 44).

Earned Leave When You Work in Vacation

  • Under the CCS (Leave) Rules, vacation staff get 5 days of earned leave on 1 January and 1 July, plus extra leave if they lose part of the vacation, up to 30 days in a calendar year (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 28(1), p. 21).
  • KVS applies this as follows: if you have at least one year of service in KVS, you get earned leave equal to 2/5 of the days you were on duty during vacations or breaks, for training, in-service courses or other duties you were specifically called for, provided the duty is not less than 10 days (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 1, p. 1).
  • Earned leave for vacation duty is capped at 30 days in a calendar year, including the 10 days you earn regularly (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 2, p. 1).
  • Journey time to attend the duty counts as duty (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 3, p. 1).
  • This applies from 14.12.2018, and old cases may be reviewed (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 4, p. 1).
  • Example: if you attend a 15-day training during summer vacation, you earn 2/5 x 15 = 6 days of earned leave.

Duty of Less Than 10 Days: Compensatory Leave

  • If the duty in vacation is less than 10 days, compensatory leave can be given instead (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 1, p. 1).
  • For extra classes, half a day's compensatory leave is given for 2 to 4 hours, and a full day for more than 4 hours (KVS Office Order on Compensatory Leave, January 2019, para (i), p. 1).
  • For escort duty or training on holidays, vacation or breaks, compensatory leave may be given whether or not you draw TA/DA (KVS Office Order on Compensatory Leave, January 2019, para (ii), p. 1).
  • Compensatory leave should normally be taken within one month of becoming due. Unused compensatory leave is not encashed at superannuation, resignation or death (KVS Office Order on Compensatory Leave, January 2019, para (iii), p. 1).

Where to Check

Check the KVS OM on vacation and breaks for the current session (KVS OM dated 19.01.2026, Vacation and Breaks 2026-27, p. 1), the KVS memorandum dated 31.08.2022 (KVS Memorandum dated 31.08.2022, para 1, p. 1) and Rule 28 of the CCS (Leave) Rules (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 28, p. 21). NVS issues its own vacation schedule and orders.

Related Questions

When is the KVS autumn break in 2026?

For most KVs listed in the 2026-27 schedule, the autumn break is from 15.10.2026 to 24.10.2026 (10 days). KVs in Ladakh, Ziro and Tawang follow separate schedules.

Do travel days count when I am called for duty in vacation?

Yes. KVS counts the period of journey for attending duty during vacation as duty.

Will I get earned leave for 5 days of duty in vacation?

Not under the 2/5 rule, which needs at least 10 days of duty. For shorter duty, compensatory leave can be given.

Do contractual teachers get vacation pay?

No. The KVS Education Code says teachers appointed on contractual basis and part-time teachers are not entitled to vacation pay (Article 67(2)).

Can unused compensatory leave be encashed?

No. KVS says no leave encashment is payable for unavailed compensatory leave at superannuation, resignation or death.
