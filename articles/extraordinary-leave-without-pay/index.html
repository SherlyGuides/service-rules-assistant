Can I Take Leave Without Pay (Extraordinary Leave)?

Central Government civil employees under the CCS (Leave) Rules, 1972Checked against official documents on 4 Oct 2026

Short answerYes. Extraordinary leave (EOL) can be granted in special circumstances when no other leave is admissible, or when other leave is admissible but you apply in writing for EOL. You get no leave salary. Temporary employees face limits of 3 to 24 months per occasion, and no leave of any kind can run continuously beyond five years.

When EOL Can Be Granted

  • EOL may be granted in special circumstances when no other leave is admissible, or when other leave is admissible but you apply in writing for EOL (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(1), p. 25).
  • You are not entitled to any leave salary during EOL (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 40(5), p. 35).
  • Leave cannot be claimed as of right and may be refused when public service needs require (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 7, p. 7).

How Long: Limits for Temporary Employees

If you are not in permanent or quasi-permanent employ, EOL on any one occasion cannot exceed these limits, unless the President decides otherwise in exceptional circumstances (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(2), p. 25):

Situation Maximum EOL on one occasion
General 3 months (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(2)(a), p. 25)
One year of continuous service and request supported by a medical certificate 6 months (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(2)(b), p. 25)
One year of continuous service and treatment for tuberculosis, leprosy, cancer or mental illness as specified 18 months (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(2)(d), p. 25)
Three years of continuous service and studies certified to be in the public interest 24 months (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(2)(e), p. 26)
  • Two spells of EOL with another kind of leave in between are treated as one continuous spell for these limits (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(5), p. 26).
  • The Head of Department may relax these limits for SC and ST employees attending notified Pre-Examination Training Courses (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(4), p. 26).
  • If EOL is given by relaxing the study limit, you must sign a bond to refund the expenditure, with interest, if you do not return to duty or you quit within three years of returning (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(3)(a), p. 26).

The Five-Year Ceiling for Everyone

  • No government servant can be granted leave of any kind for a continuous period of more than five years (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 12(1), p. 8).
  • If you stay absent for more than five years continuously, with or without leave (other than on foreign service), you are deemed to have resigned, unless the President decides otherwise. You must first get a reasonable opportunity to explain (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 12(2), p. 8).
  • This does not apply where leave is applied for on medical certificate in connection with a disability (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 12(2), proviso, p. 8).

Effects of EOL on Your Leave

  • Your next half-yearly earned leave credit is reduced by one-tenth of the EOL period, up to 15 days (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 27(3), p. 20).
  • The leave sanctioning authority may retrospectively convert periods of absence without leave into EOL (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32(6), p. 26).
  • If you stay absent after your leave ends without an extension, you get no leave salary for that period. It is debited as half pay leave to the extent due, and the rest is treated as EOL (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 25(1), p. 18). Wilful absence can lead to disciplinary action (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 25(2), p. 18).
  • EOL granted on medical certificate or otherwise may be converted retrospectively into leave not due, subject to Rule 31 (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 10, Note, p. 8). A request to convert leave must reach the authority within 30 days of rejoining duty (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 10(1), proviso, p. 8).
  • Leave preparatory to retirement cannot include EOL (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 38(1), Note, p. 27).

Where to Check

Read Rules 12, 27 and 32 of the CCS (Leave) Rules, 1972 in the DoPT consolidated copy updated as on 24.09.2024 (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 32, p. 25).

These rules do not apply to Railway servants (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 2(a), p. 3). KVS, NVS and Delhi Police staff should also check their own organisation's orders.

Related Questions

Do I get any salary during extraordinary leave?

No. A government servant on extraordinary leave is not entitled to any leave salary (Rule 40(5)).

Can I take EOL even if I have earned leave?

Yes, if you apply in writing for EOL. It can be granted when other leave is admissible but you specifically ask for EOL in writing (Rule 32(1)(b)).

Can unauthorised absence be regularised as EOL?

Yes. The authority competent to grant leave may retrospectively convert periods of absence without leave into extraordinary leave (Rule 32(6)).

Does EOL reduce my earned leave?

Yes. The earned leave credit at the start of the next half-year is reduced by one-tenth of the EOL period, up to 15 days (Rule 27(3)).

What is the longest leave I can take at one stretch?

No leave of any kind can be granted for a continuous period of more than five years (Rule 12(1)).
