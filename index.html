Can I take earned leave during probation?

Yes. Under the central rules, a probationer is entitled to leave, including earned leave, "as if he had held his post substantively otherwise than on probation" (CCS (Leave) Rules 1972, Rule 33(1)(a), PDF p. 26).

  • Leave is not a right. Your sanctioning authority can refuse it or cancel it if work requires (Rule 7, PDF p. 7).
  • If your services are being terminated, leave can't go beyond the end of your probation or the termination date (Rule 33(1)(b), PDF pp. 26–27).

How it works

It looks the rule up before it answers.

  1. You ask in your own wordsType a question, or attach a photo, PDF or Word file of an order or notice.
  2. It searches the libraryRules, office memoranda, manuals and court judgments, and reads the relevant provisions in full.
  3. It answers with the sourceEach statement names the rule and the page, and the link opens the official PDF at that page.
  4. It says what it doesn't haveIf the official document isn't in its library, it says so instead of guessing.

Good to know

What it is, and what it isn't.

Information, not legal advice

It explains what the rules say. For a decision that matters, check the cited rule yourself, and take advice on serious matters such as a penalty or a court case.

Not a government website

This is an independent project. It is not run by, or affiliated with, the Government of India or any department.

Your organisation's own rules

Some employers adopt the central rules with changes. Where that may apply, the assistant says so and tells you where to confirm.

Questions are recorded

Questions, attached files and the internet address they come from are recorded to check and improve answers. Leave out names and personal details you don't need to share.

Answers are written by AI

Answers come from AI models (Anthropic's Claude, and Google's Gemini as a backup), working only from the library above. They can still make mistakes; the citations let you check.

Still being tested

It is in early use and improving. If an answer is wrong or a document is missing, that is useful to know.
