Questions Only Practitioners Can Settle

The written rules are unclear or out of date on these. If you handle them at work, tell us.

  1. Do teachers (vacation staff) get half pay leave, given Rule 29(1) of the CCS (Leave) Rules as substituted in 2018?
  2. Is KVS still crediting earned leave at 2/5 of vacation days worked (memo of 31.08.2022)?
  3. Who actually decides Delhi Police appeals against DCP orders today: Additional CP or Joint CP?
  4. Has the special air LTC scheme (to 25.09.2026) been extended?
  5. How many days of casual leave do Delhi DoE teachers and guest teachers actually get, and under which order?
